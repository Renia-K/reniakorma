Artificial Territories

Assistant Curator

Das LOT, Vienna, Austria — May 2025

[ARTIFICIAL] TERRITORIES is an interactive transmedia exhibition exploring the creative and critical possibilities of artificial intelligence in media art. Featuring 12 interactive works by 23 artists, the exhibition invites visitors to connect, play, and shape the future of AI-driven creativity within an immersive, labyrinthine space at Das LOT.

Building on the success of [LUDIC] TERRITORIES, this edition expands the conversation by focusing on artworks that use AI not only as a tool but also as a subject of critical reflection—exploring ethics, creativity, and authorship in the digital age.

As Assistant Curator, Renia Korma contributed to conceptual development, coordinated with participating artists, and supported the design of the interactive visitor experience. Her role involved shaping the exhibition narrative to encourage active participation and critical engagement with AI’s role in contemporary art.

For more information, visit the official exhibition page: https://www.lot.wien/artificialterritories.
